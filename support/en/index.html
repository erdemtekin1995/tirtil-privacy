Frequently asked

  • How do I cancel the subscription? On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period.
  • I changed phones and my subscription is not showing. Parents' area → Subscription → "Restore purchases".
  • Does reading progress move to a new device? No. The app has no accounts; progress and voice recordings stay on the device only.
  • How do I delete the voice recordings? Parents' area → Data on this device → "Delete voice recordings".
  • What is the parental gate? The short multiplication question shown before settings and the purchase screen; it keeps children from entering these sections by accident.
