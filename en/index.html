Tırtıl Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Terms · Support · Türkçe

Tırtıl is a bilingual (Turkish and English) picture-book app for children aged 0–5. This page explains what information the app handles and where it stays. Because the app is made for young children, we chose the simplest rule: we do not collect any personal information about your child.

In short

  • Tırtıl has no server. There are no accounts and no sign-in; the app never asks for a name, email address, date of birth or photo.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking software.
  • Reading progress and voice recordings stay only on your own device.
  • The only time the app goes online is to verify a subscription.

Information kept on your device

While the app is used, the following is stored on the device only:

  • Which page of which book was reached, which books were finished and the stars collected
  • The chosen age group, reading language, interface language and appearance settings
  • The audio recorded when your child uses the "Record" section to read a page aloud

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. In the parents' area you can delete the voice recordings and reset the reading progress; deleting the app removes everything from the device.

Microphone and voice recordings

The microphone is used only when your child wants to read a page in their own voice and listen to it, and the system asks for your permission at that moment. The recording stays in the device's own file storage; it is not backed up, uploaded or shared with anyone. If you decline, the rest of the app works as usual.

Subscription

The subscription is sold through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. A service called RevenueCat is used to verify the subscription status. The information sent to it is limited to:

  • An anonymous identifier specific to this installation of the app, which does not identify a person
  • The purchase record from the store (which plan, when, whether it is active)
  • Information technically required to make the connection (IP address, device model, operating-system version)

No advertising identifier is collected. This information is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold. RevenueCat's own policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

The subscription screen and every link that leaves the app are behind a parental gate.

Children's privacy

Tırtıl is directed to children and does not knowingly collect, request or share personal information from children. The app has no chat, comments, sharing or contact with other users. Purchases, settings and external links are behind a gate that only an adult can pass.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is no account to delete. You can remove the information on the device from the parents' area or by deleting the app. You manage and cancel your subscription in the subscription settings of your store account. For questions under GDPR, COPPA, KVKK or similar laws, you can write to us.

Changes

If this text changes, the new version is published on this page and the date above is updated. If a feature that collects data is ever added, we will state it here clearly first.
